# Sagittarius A* discovery deck — sources and asset boundaries

This declaration separates SlideBlocks' first-party implementation and scientific narration from astronomy images, paper material, data, fonts, and icons supplied by others. It is a provenance record, not a legal conclusion.

## First-party layer

The deck's Slidev/Vue implementation, original arrangement, interactive explanations, locally authored diagrams, and original narration created by SlideBlocks contributors are covered by the repository MIT License, only to the extent those contributors have the right to license them.

The MIT License does not override the terms for source papers, datasets, astronomy images, fonts, icons, or any generated output containing them.

## Scientific sources

The deck's 21-item bibliography is maintained in `components/ReferencesIndex.vue`. Key machine-readable source links include:

- [GRAVITY Collaboration (2020)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.07187)
- [Schödel et al. (2002)](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0210426)
- [Gillessen et al. (2009)](https://arxiv.org/abs/0810.4674)
- [EHT Collaboration (2022), First Sagittarius A* Results I](https://doi.org/10.3847/2041-8213/ac6674)

Citations establish scientific attribution. They do not by themselves establish permission to copy a paper figure, table, caption, dataset, or substantial text. Each plotted parameter and each redrawn or adapted figure still requires a source-to-slide record and human confirmation.
