# Report / Briefing Visual Direction

Read this when the Report / Briefing direction fits the audience, materials, and viewing context. It supports work updates, reviews, and decision briefs without prescribing their task or narrative. Explicit brand/template instructions and the accepted deck remain controlling. Use the existing page plan and visual grammar; do not create another design document.

## What the public examples contribute

These observations come from rendered content pages, not template covers. Page numbers are one-based PDF pages. The synthesis below is a starting direction, not a verified SlideBlocks Registry profile or a copy of any company's identity.

| Source | Observed visual method | Adaptation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Tesla Q2 2025](https://www.tesla.com/sites/default/files/downloads/TSLA-Q2-2025-Update.pdf), pp. 5, 7, 19, 21 | Stable alignment across metric explanations; compact operating-state comparison; a real construction photograph fills the canvas; repeated metric panels share a grammar. | Align each result with the explanation that qualifies it. Give actual deliverable evidence meaningful scale. Keep comparable metrics visually comparable. Enlarge its tiny investor-oriented text for a spoken update. |
| [Tencent 2024 Q4 / annual results](https://static.www.tencent.com/uploads/2025/03/19/f55938d61be94cf9700a971a4db08809.pdf), pp. 8, 14–15 | Parallel workstreams have distinct aligned regions; related segments retain the same colors across time; quarterly and annual views are visibly separated. | Keep workstream identity and semantic colors stable. Separate time bases and distinguish total, composition, and change rather than stacking unrelated numbers into one dashboard. |
| [Wikimedia Reading Q1 2015–16](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:WMF_Reading_Quarterly_Review_Q1_2015-16.pdf), pp. 2, 20–23, 36 | Objectives, success measures, and actual status are adjacent; campaign context is visible in the time series; a disappointing result is stated directly; next-period goals include dependencies. | Make target/actual/status easy to scan together. Put qualifiers beside the result they limit. Show the actual intervention or period when supplied. Its dated gradients, dense grids, and dual-axis styling are not defaults. |
| [NASA Roman status, March 2024](https://science.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/kruk-roman-apac-march2024.pdf), pp. 2–6 | The current point is marked on the project schedule; component status is grounded in annotated hardware photographs. | Give progress a visible reference point and connect claims of completion to the actual output. Reduce schedule detail for projection; keep unknown dates unknown. |

## Form a coherent report grammar

Build hierarchy around the audience's current judgment: the important observation or state, the material that establishes it, and the qualifier or next action. Let a small set of shared anchors carry the deck: title position, content alignment, type roles, metric/period labels, source treatment, and state encoding.

Use a limited palette with roles. A restrained light canvas, dark ink, and one emphasis color can be a useful starting point. Add a distinct exception color only when it identifies a real risk or blocker; pair it with explicit status text. A dark or branded direction can use the same reasoning. Do not assign scientific work and business work permanent colors or fonts.

Keep the evidence visually central, but choose its form from the reporting question. A report can be led by an annotated output, aligned state comparison, meaningful schedule, or honest quantitative chart. A scientific mechanism diagram, giant numeral, stock image, or card grid does not establish progress merely by filling the canvas.

Use ordinary bullets for short explanations. Keep projection-readable contrast and text; do not imitate Tesla's tiny gray prose or Tencent's dense investor tables. Reserve visible boundaries for meaningful grouping, comparison, or state. Avoid repeating badges, corner labels, bilingual title duplicates, or decorative rules.

## Choose a carrier from the judgment and available evidence

These are examples, not a closed menu or mandatory page sequence. Preserve fixed page content and order.

| Audience question | Useful visual expression | Condition and truth boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where does the work stand? | An aligned state comparison or milestone sequence with the current point emphasized. | With dates and a real schedule baseline, a compact timeline may help. With only work states, use those states rather than invented durations, percentages, or progress-bar lengths. |
| What changed versus the plan or previous period? | A shared-scale actual/target chart, before/after comparison, or repeated small multiples. | Preserve comparable definitions, denominators, time windows, and units. Distinguish actuals from forecasts. A single snapshot cannot supply a trend. |
| What was delivered? | A large real screenshot, output excerpt, field photograph, or test artifact with local annotations. | Use actual project evidence. Public stock imagery or a generated dashboard cannot prove internal delivery. With no factual image, use the supplied state or measurement honestly. |
| What does this measurement establish? | A directly labelled count/composition/relationship display next to its interpretation boundary. | Make units and scope recoverable without narration. A replay count or percentage is not project completion, adoption, reliability, or business benefit unless the source establishes that meaning. |
| What is blocking progress? | A short dependency view or aligned issue/impact/handling comparison with the real blocker emphasized. | Distinguish a current blocker from a possible future risk. Show a causal arrow only when the dependency is supplied or established. Do not fabricate an owner or resolution date. |
| What should happen next? | A focused action and owner, with the conditional next steps or decision consequences nearby. | Prioritize the actual ask; do not manufacture one. Show unconfirmed dates and unsatisfied prerequisites as such. |

For a mechanism, process, or chart that needs a diagram/plot, retain the shared production routing in `diagram-direction.md`; this reference chooses the communication form, not another drawing engine. Keep figures and annotations editable where the selected route supports it.

## Check whether visual adaptation did useful work

Compare the rendered output with a uniform text/table treatment using the same facts. Look for a substantive change in scan order, comparison cost, progress location, evidence scale, or decision visibility. Changing the palette, font, background, or section names alone is not sufficient evidence of content-sensitive expression.

The real blocker must remain as visible as the completed work. A visually dominant result must carry its denominator and scope. A sparse page still needs enough material to answer its question; a dense page must not hide the decision in an exhaustive log. Check the complete normal, reduced-motion, print, and offline states through the shared QA workflow. A successful example demonstrates that direction on that material; repeat across different materials and templates before claiming general routing reliability.
